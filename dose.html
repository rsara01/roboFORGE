Environment

Switch the world the dose robot drives in. Hospital has a bed, supply table and bedside drop zone; flat field is an empty sandbox.

Reset

Returns the chassis to its spawn pose and re-zeros the SCARA arm.

Controls

W/S or ↑/↓ drive forward / reverse.
A/D or ←/→ turn left / right.
Q/E rotate shoulder (q1).
I/K rotate elbow (q2).
T/G raise / lower the whole arm on the mast.
R/F raise / lower the wrist Z.
Z/X close / open the gripper.

Motion Recording

Record motion, play it back, then export a coordinate matrix plus an Arduino sketch driving BLDC ESCs. Time scale only goes down (1.0x = recorded speed; 0.1x = slow-motion).
